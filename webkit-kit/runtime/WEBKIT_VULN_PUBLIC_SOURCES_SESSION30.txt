4) Project Zero JITSploitation I: https://projectzero.google/2020/09/jitsploitation-one.html
- Author Samuel Groß, 2020-09-01.
- CVE-2020-9802 was fixed in iOS 13.5; CVE-2020-9870 and CVE-2020-9910 were mitigation bypasses fixed in iOS 13.6.
- CVE-2020-9802 is a JSC DFG CSE bug: DFGClobberize failed to include ArithMode for ArithNegate (and also ArithAbs), allowing a checked operation to be substituted with an unchecked one. The patch URL is https://github.com/WebKit/webkit/commit/951d27d5ba08b6c29370b05dc6b4ffe18be1ca18 and changes `def(PureValue(node))` to `def(PureValue(node, node->arithMode()))`.
- The Project Zero article states the bug can yield an out-of-bounds read/write on the JSC heap, and explains DFG/FTL/JIT components. These facts are upstream/iOS research, not PS4 evidence.
