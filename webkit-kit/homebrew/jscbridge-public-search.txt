Public source follow-up:

CTurt's article https://cturt.github.io/ps4-3.html states that libSceJitBridge.sprx was reverse engineered and that a simple `allocateJIT` wrapper was added to PS4-SDK. The cited source is https://github.com/CTurt/PS4-SDK/blob/master/libPS4/source/jit.c. The repository is archived (Oct 13, 2019); its jit.c dynamically resolves sceKernelJitCreateSharedMemory, sceKernelJitCreateAliasOfSharedMemory and sceKernelJitMapSharedMemory, then maps executable/writable aliases. It is an application-side wrapper for proprietary runtime exports, not the JSCBridge VM/compiler provider required by WebKit-601-1300. It does not contain JSCBridge.h, Memory.h, PoolAllocator16K, VTableMap, VirtualMethodCall, JscBridge_vm or SceJitBridge implementation.
